# R08 — The Last of Us Part II accessibility

**Study focus:** accessibility as a designed system of options, not a late toggle.

## Player task
Play with vision/hearing/motor/cognitive needs met.

## Hierarchy of a11y craft
1. Options exist early (not buried)  
2. High contrast / screen reader / remapping / subtitle control  
3. Combat assists as first-class  
4. Defaults sane; power users go deeper  

## Why it feels pro
- A11y changes layout/input paths by design.  
- Multiple channels for same info (audio + visual).  
- Industry benchmark for *ambition* — not your minimum scope.

## Steal
- Plan text scale, contrast mode, reduce motion before wiring UI.  
- Pair every color signal with shape.  
- Document which risks your game actually has.

## Do not copy
- Claiming “we have TLOU2 a11y” with one checkbox.  
- Shipping 60 options you can’t test.
