# R01 — Doom Eternal HUD (chaos readability)

**Study focus:** ammo, resources, and status stay parseable during extreme motion and VFX.

## Player task
Survive and shoot while the screen is visually loud.

## Hierarchy (typical)
1. Crosshair / center (gaze) — clear  
2. Ammo / weapon state — high contrast edge  
3. Health / armor — persistent, shape-readable  
4. Glory kill / ability prompts — contextual flash  

## Why it feels pro
- Critical numbers survive muzzle flash and enemy spam.  
- Silhouette of meters works even if color washes out.  
- Non-essential chrome stays thin.

## Steal for your game
- Stress-test UI on your busiest VFX moment.  
- Prefer solid chip/panel behind numeric over outline-only.  
- Keep center mostly free during core verb.

## Do not copy
Demon aesthetic, specific icon art, layout pixel positions.
